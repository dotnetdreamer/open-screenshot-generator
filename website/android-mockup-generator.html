Device frames

What the frame picker holds

Three phone chassis cover the Android landscape: a punch hole flagship, a notch design and a classic status bar phone, plus 7 inch and 10 inch tablet frames. Each masks your capture to the display, and a 3D Android element with preset poses covers hero shots. A frame can also swap between its Android and iPhone counterparts, so one layout serves both stores

Three steps

How it works

  • 1. Open the editor. The editor runs in your browser with nothing to install and no account to create. Pick a ready made template or start from a blank canvas at the exact Google Play size
  • 2. Drop in your screenshot. Upload a raw screen capture and it snaps into the device frame, masked to the screen with the right corner radius. Switch frames or turn on a 3D pose any time

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Is the Android mockup generator really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no watermark and no export limit. The editor is open source and everything renders in your browser

Which Android frames are included?

A punch hole flagship, a notch phone and a classic status bar phone, plus 7 inch and 10 inch tablets. Google requires generic frames rather than branded devices, so these neutral chassis are exactly what a Play listing needs

What size should a Play Store screenshot be?

Google accepts anything from 320 to 3840 pixels per side, with a 16:9 or 9:16 ratio recommended. The editor defaults to 1080x1920 portrait, which is the accepted safe answer for phone listings

Does it make the 1024x500 feature graphic too?

Yes. The feature graphic is its own board type with its own templates, so one project holds your screenshots and the banner Google needs before it will show your promo video
