Credit where due

When Previewed is the better pick

  • You want cinematic 3D animation. Their rotating, floating device renders and animated promo videos are genuinely beautiful, and nothing here matches that level of polish yet
  • You want social media template breadth. Their library goes well past store screenshots into social posts and marketing shots, if you can accept devices from 2022
  • Ten renders are all you need. Plus is a $9.99 one time purchase with 10 exports at 1080p and up, no subscription attached, which is a fair deal for a single burst of marketing assets

The other side

When Open Screenshot Generator wins

  • You need current devices. Store visitors see this year's hardware in your listing. The newest phone at Previewed is the iPhone 14, added in December 2022
  • Your screenshots must upload at full size. Apple's required iPhone tier is 1290 by 2796 pixels, far past a 720p cap. Exports here are always full resolution, for free
  • You want to own your exports. No Creative Commons attribution, no commercial use gate, no license text to read. What you export is yours
  • You want a maintained tool. New device frames and features ship here regularly, in the open, with the whole history public on GitHub
  • You want preview videos at store specs. Drop a screen recording into a frame and export an MP4 in Chrome, Edge or the desktop app, conformed to what App Store Connect accepts

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Is Previewed abandoned?

Only its makers know. The public signals as of August 2026, a changelog frozen at v1.1.8 from February 2023, a footer copyright still reading 2021, a blog subdomain that no longer resolves and no device frame newer than the iPhone 14, all point the same way. The renders it produces still look lovely

What does the Previewed free tier actually allow?

2D exports up to 720p, under a Creative Commons attribution license, with commercial use reserved for paid plans. Plus is a $9.99 one time purchase with 10 exports at 1080p and up, and Pro is $19 per month billed annually at $228

Is 720p usable for App Store screenshots?

No. The required 6.9 inch iPhone tier is 1290 by 2796 pixels, and every other size Apple and Google accept is also far past 720p, so a 720p export cannot be uploaded as a store screenshot at all

Is Open Screenshot Generator really completely free?

Yes. There is no paid tier at all. Exports come out at full resolution with no attribution license, no watermark, no account and no export limit. The full source code is public on GitHub

Can it match Previewed's 3D animations?

Honestly, no. You get 3D device poses on current hardware and MP4 preview videos in Chrome, Edge or the desktop app, which covers what the stores need. Previewed's cinematic animation renders remain nicer to look at, on the devices it has
