What the frame picker holds

Two current iPad chassis, the 13 inch iPad Pro and the 11 inch iPad, both with the slim even bezel of the modern lineup. Your capture is masked to the display with the correct corner radius. Boards can sit next to your iPhone set in the same project, so one file holds the whole listing

Three steps

How it works

  • 1. Open the editor. The editor runs in your browser with nothing to install and no account to create. Pick a ready made template or start from a blank canvas at the exact App Store size
  • 2. Drop in your screenshot. Upload a raw screen capture and it snaps into the device frame, masked to the screen with the right corner radius. Switch frames or turn on a 3D pose any time
  • 3. Export store ready files. Export PNG files at every size your store listing needs. Everything renders locally, so your screenshots never leave your machine

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Is the iPad mockup generator really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no watermark and no export limit. The editor is open source and everything renders in your browser

Do I need separate iPad screenshots for the App Store?

Yes, if your app runs on iPad. Apple requires a 13 inch iPad set and scales it to the smaller iPad classes. The editor starts iPad boards at 2064x2752 so the export is accepted first try

Which iPad frames are included?

The 13 inch iPad Pro and the 11 inch iPad, both in the modern slim bezel design with your capture masked to the display shape

Can iPhone and iPad boards live in one project?

Yes. A project holds boards at mixed sizes, so your iPhone set, iPad set and feature graphic export together from one file
