02 / Tool by tool

When to pick
each tool

Each of these tools is good at something. Here's when we'd pick each one, and when we'd pick ours instead

AppScreens

A web app for screenshot sets, paid by the month or the year

$99 a yearPro, or $25 month to month

AppScreens runs in your browser and gets updated often. The paid plans translate into 80+ languages, add 3D and clay frames, and send your screenshots to both stores in one click. The free plan is for trying AppScreens on your own app: one project, five screens, four templates, no translations and no uploads. AppScreens doesn't make preview videos

Pick AppScreens if

  • You also use Apple's custom product pages or store page tests
  • You need more languages than our 57, up to 80+
  • You want every project saved in the cloud, with nothing to install

Pick ours if

  • You want translations, 3D frames and store uploads without paying
  • You want preview videos from the same app
  • You make screenshots for clients, which the AppScreens free plan doesn't allow

Checked on 19 November 2026 at appscreens.com/pricing and the free plan guide

AppLaunchpad

A web app for screenshot sets, with a big library

$10 a monthPro, paid yearly, or $15 month to month

AppLaunchpad runs in your browser and has a big library on Pro: 1,000+ templates, 10,000+ graphics and 700+ fonts. You need an account to start. AI resizes your design for every device, and Pro translates your text with AI. You download the files and upload them to the stores yourself, and AppLaunchpad doesn't make videos

Pick AppLaunchpad if

  • You want a huge library of templates, graphics and fonts
  • You'd like 3D phones held in a hand

Pick ours if

  • You want to send the set straight to both stores
  • You want preview videos too
  • You'd like to start without signing up

Checked on 19 November 2026 at theapplaunchpad.com/pricing and theapplaunchpad.com

Previewed

2D and 3D mockups and promo videos, in the browser

$19 a monthPro, paid yearly. Plus is $9.99 once

Previewed makes 2D and 3D mockups and short promo videos of your app in the browser. The free plan exports small 720p images for personal use, and you have to credit Previewed. You need an account to download. The newest phone frame is the iPhone 14, and the Previewed changelog hasn't changed since February 2023

Pick Previewed if

  • You want 3D animations and short promo videos of your app
  • You only need a few large images, and $9.99 once covers 10 of them

Pick ours if

  • You need full size store images without paying
  • You want this year's phones, like the iPhone 17 and 18
  • You'd like to use your screenshots for business without crediting anyone

Checked on 19 November 2026 at previewed.app/pricing and previewed.app/plans

Screenshots.pro

A web app for screenshot sets, with a free plan

$19 a monthStandard, or $149 a year

Screenshots.pro runs in your browser, and the free plan has every device and template. 3D angles and translations need the Standard plan. On the free plan, you have to credit Screenshots.pro with a link wherever your screenshots appear, App Store and Google Play included. The newest entry on the Screenshots.pro changelog is from October 2024

Pick Screenshots.pro if

  • You'd like to hire a designer for your set through the same site
  • You want an API that makes screenshots on its servers, on the Extended plan

Pick ours if

  • You want 3D phones and translations without paying
  • You'd rather not put a credit link on your store page
  • You also want preview videos and uploads to both stores

Checked on 19 November 2026 at screenshots.pro/pricing and its license page

AppMockUp

A free screenshot editor in the browser

FreePaid templates are $9.97 each

AppMockUp Studio is free, needs no account and doesn't upload your screenshots. Your project is a file you save on your computer, with no autosave. 16 templates are free and 64 cost $9.97 each. AppMockUp has no Mac or Apple Watch frames, no translations and no videos

Pick AppMockUp if

  • You want AppMockUp's mesh gradient and pattern backgrounds
  • You only make phone and tablet screenshots and like a small, simple editor

Pick ours if

  • You need Mac or Apple Watch screenshots too
  • You want your text translated, and preview videos
  • You want to send the set straight to the stores

Checked on 19 November 2026 at app-mockup.com and its FAQ

Rotato

3D mockups and videos of your app, on the Mac and the web

$79 onceMac app. $89 and $199 plans too

Rotato makes realistic 3D mockups and animated videos of your app. The Mac app is paid once and keeps working forever, with 6 or 12 months of updates. Exports from the free trial carry a red Rotato tag. Rotato can render every App Store size in one go. Rotato's own site says the app specializes in device mockups. Rotato Web runs in a browser on a subscription

Pick Rotato if

  • You want realistic 3D animations of your app, up to 8K on Premium
  • You need videos with a transparent background

Pick ours if

  • You want finished screenshot sets with headlines, not only device scenes
  • You want a free app for Windows or Linux
  • You want your screenshots translated and sent to the stores

Checked on 19 November 2026 at rotato.app/pricing and Rotato Web pricing

Hotpot

Free screenshot templates in the browser, next to a set of paid AI tools

FreeAI uses credits, from $10 a month

Hotpot's screenshot templates are free to edit and download, with no watermark, and businesses can use them too. Hotpot has a huge template library with the newest phones. AI designs cost credits. Hotpot's screenshot pages don't mention translations or videos, and you upload the files to the stores yourself

Pick Hotpot if

  • You want a much bigger choice of free templates
  • You'd like Hotpot to make a new template for you, for free

Pick ours if

  • You want your screenshots in other languages
  • You want real 3D phones you can turn to any angle
  • You want to send the finished set straight to both stores

Checked on 19 November 2026 at hotpot.ai/app-store-screenshot-generator and hotpot.ai/pricing

ButterKit

A Mac app for screenshot sets, with real 3D devices

$49 a yearOr $99 once, or $10 a month

ButterKit is a Mac app that gets updated often. Your files stay on your Mac, and you don't need an account. ButterKit has real 3D devices, translations on your Mac or through your own AI key, and a connection for coding agents like Claude Code. Free exports carry a Made with ButterKit watermark, and sending to App Store Connect needs Pro

Pick ButterKit if

  • You want an app made only for the Mac
  • You'd like to manage your App Store text from the same app, on Pro

Pick ours if

  • You want free exports with no watermark
  • You work on Windows or Linux, or in a browser
  • You send screenshots to Google Play too

Checked on 19 November 2026 at butterkit.app/pricing and butterkit.app

Figma

A design app for any screen, in the browser and on your computer

$16 a monthProfessional, paid yearly, or $20 month to month

Many app designers already work in Figma. You get exact control over every pixel, and other people share free screenshot templates and plugins. You set up the store sizes, the phone frames and the file names yourself, or find a plugin for each one

Pick Figma if

  • Your app's design already lives in Figma
  • You know your way around design tools and want control over every pixel
  • Your team already pays for Figma

Pick ours if

  • You'd rather start from a finished screenshot set than from empty frames
  • You want store sizes, phone frames and languages built in, with no plugins to find
  • You want to send the set to both stores without leaving the app

Checked on 19 November 2026 at figma.com/pricing and the Starter plan guide

03 / Open Screenshot Generator

What you get
for $0

The same editor runs in your browser and on your computer. The desktop app adds the parts that need your computer or your store accounts

In your browser

Open the editor and start. You don't need an account

  • Every template and design tool
  • 57 languages, translated for free or by your own AI
  • PNG files at every store size, with no watermark
  • Preview videos, in Chrome or Edge
  • AI that makes a first design, using an AI account you already have
  • Editing with other people, once you sign in
  • Saving to your own Google Drive or GitHub

The desktop app adds

Free for Mac, Windows and Linux

  • Sending your screenshots straight to App Store Connect and Google Play
  • Screenshots of your app from the iPhone Simulator or an Android emulator, in every language
  • Your reviews and star ratings from both stores, in one list
  • Claude Code or Codex, on the plan you already pay for

Where ours falls short

Every tool has limits, and these are ours

  • Your projects are saved in your browser until you sign in or connect Google Drive or GitHub. Clearing the browser's data deletes them
  • To export videos in a browser, you need Chrome or Edge
  • Sending to the stores needs the desktop app
  • The free translator is shared by everyone, so a big job takes a while. Your own AI is faster
  • The Mac App Store version can't run Claude Code or Codex. The Mac app from GitHub can

A few
useful answers

Read the documentation ↗
Is Open Screenshot Generator really free?

Yes. Every template and design tool is free, with no watermark and no export limit. The code is public on GitHub under the MIT license. If you connect an AI service, that service may charge you for it

What does open source mean?

The code that runs the app is public. Anyone can read it, check what it does and run a copy of their own

Do I need to know design?

No. Start from a template, drop in screenshots of your app and change the words. You can also ask the AI to make a first design, then change anything you like

Can I use the editor on Windows or Linux?

Yes. Use the web editor in your browser, or get the desktop app from the Microsoft Store or GitHub. GitHub has the app for Mac and Linux too

Which tool should I pick?

If you want everything free, with no watermark, start with ours. If you'd rather pay for a web app that keeps every project in the cloud, look at AppScreens or AppLaunchpad. If you want realistic 3D videos of your app, Rotato is made for that. If your app's design already lives in Figma, Figma works too, but you'll set up the sizes and phone frames yourself

How up to date is this page?

We checked every tool on 19 November 2026. Prices and plans change, so check each tool's price page before you pay. If something here is out of date, tell us on GitHub
