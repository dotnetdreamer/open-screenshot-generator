Device frames

What the frame picker holds

The frame picker covers iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15, iPhone 14, iPhone 13, iPhone X and a classic bezel iPhone. Every frame masks your screenshot to the display shape, including the corner radius and the cutout. For hero shots there is also a 3D iPhone element with preset poses and adjustable angles

Three steps

How it works

  • 1. Open the editor. The editor runs in your browser with nothing to install and no account to create. Pick a ready made template or start from a blank canvas at the exact App Store size
  • 2. Drop in your screenshot. Upload a raw screen capture and it snaps into the device frame, masked to the screen with the right corner radius. Switch frames or turn on a 3D pose any time
  • 3. Export store ready files. Export PNG files at every size your store listing needs. Everything renders locally, so your screenshots never leave your machine

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Is the iPhone mockup generator really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no watermark and no export limit. The editor is open source and everything renders in your browser

Which iPhone frames are included?

iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15, iPhone 14, iPhone 13, iPhone X and a classic bezel iPhone as flat frames, plus a 3D iPhone element with preset poses for hero shots

What size should an iPhone App Store screenshot be?

Upload the 6.9 inch tier, which accepts 1290x2796, 1260x2736 or 1320x2868 in portrait. App Store Connect scales that tier down for the smaller display classes, so one good set covers every iPhone

Can I use the mockups outside the App Store?

Yes. The canvas takes any custom size, so the same frames work for social posts, a landing page hero or a press kit. Export is plain PNG, yours to use anywhere
