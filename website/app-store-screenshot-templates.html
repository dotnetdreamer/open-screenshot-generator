Questions

Template questions, answered

Are these App Store screenshot templates really free?

Yes. All of them open in the free editor with no account, no watermark and no export limits. The whole project is open source, so the templates and the editor behind them are free to use and free to inspect

How do I use a template?

Click any template and it opens in the editor as a new project. Drop your own screenshots into the device frames, change the captions and colors, then export every size your store listing needs from the same project

Which sizes do the templates export?

Every size both stores require. iPhone sets export the required 1290x2796 tier and the other accepted iPhone sizes, iPad sets export 2064x2752, the Google Play sets export 1080x1920 alongside the 1024x500 feature graphic, Mac sets export 2560x1600 and Apple Watch sets export the 422x514 baseline size

Can I turn my own design into a template?

Yes. Start from a blank canvas, design your set, then export the project as JSON and reuse it any time. The template catalog itself is open source, so you can also contribute a template to the project on GitHub

Do the app preview video templates really export MP4?

Yes. Drop a screen recording into the phone frame and export an MP4 conformed to what App Store Connect accepts. Video export runs in Chrome, Edge or the desktop app
