## 3. License scope notes

- The Adobe Stock standard license permits worldwide, perpetual commercial use
  in designs, apps, websites, and marketing material, including composited
  screenshot exports from this app. Attribution is not required.
- The standard license does not permit distributing a Stock asset in a way
  whose *primary purpose* is for third parties to extract it as a standalone
  file. These palette assets exist to compose the app's exports (composite
  works). If Open Screenshot Generator is ever marketed as a general-purpose stock/asset
  marketplace, revisit this: <https://stock.adobe.com/license-terms>.
- To remove or swap an asset: delete its file from
  `public/elements/images/<group>/` and its entry in `src/lib/imageLibrary.ts`.

## 5. Original illustrations (`mascots/`, `objects/`, `stickers/`, `products/`, `cards/`, `drawings/`, `sketches/`, `line-art/`, `emoji/`)

The Mascots, 3D objects, Stickers, Products, Cards, Drawings and Line art
groups are original artwork drawn for this project (SVG and HTML written for
the Hoppi, Expertly, Roamo, Formpal, Sunup, Wishmint, Lavish, Postie, Hummi,
Signsy, Traceberry, Paylark, Umdo, Parsnap, Tenpost, Askadeck, Gabwise, Kinloom
and Kanapon templates, rendered to PNG and trimmed, and 3D scenes written for
the Owedly template).
The Signsy signer, the people in Line art, the Gabwise heads and the Kinloom
avatars are invented cartoon people, not likenesses of anyone. The Umdo box
robot, the Gabwise chat bubbles and the Kanapon daruma are invented characters;
the daruma is drawn after the traditional Japanese doll of that name. None of
it is stock or traced. The Owedly coins, coin pile, scales, money bag, receipt,
party popper and flying cash (object-coin-claim to object-coin-emerald-edge,
object-coins-gold-pile, object-scales-gold, object-money-bag, object-receipt,
object-party-popper and object-winged-cash) are 3D models built in code and
rendered with three.js (MIT License); the pile's coins were dropped and settled
with the Rapier physics engine (Apache License 2.0), which is not part of this
repository. Their "$" is the glyph of Outfit, and the banknote is an invented
design, not real currency. The lilac clay objects (object-clay-arrow,
object-clay-coin, object-clay-robot, object-clay-puzzle, object-clay-bubble,
object-clay-percent and object-clay-shield), the chrome lips, heart and
sparkle (object-chrome-lips, object-chrome-heart and object-chrome-sparkle)
and the pearl heart (object-heart-pearl) are 3D models built in code for the
Plumfolio and Lulu templates and rendered with Blender's Cycles renderer
(Blender is under the GNU GPL, which puts no terms on the pictures it
renders). Their dollar sign and percent sign are built from rods and rings,
not taken from a font. The pictures on the Hushwave screens (the marbled
colour, the ocean, the ridges, the sunflower head and the spiral of coloured
marks) are generated in code, with no photo in them. The app icons in 3D objects
are invented designs, not the icons of real apps. Three cards build on library
photos from section 1: card-person.png is girls-as622285813 in black and white,
and card-dance.png and card-yourself.png use guys-as211660510 and
girls-as608612382 as one-colour silhouettes. The Sketches group is made from
section 1 photos only: sketch-portrait-1 to -7 are pencil-sketch versions of
girls-as568002174, girls-as608612382, guys-as476183094, education-as436829852,
girls-as622285813, guys-as488088494 and education-as268560315, in that order,
and sketch-painted-1 to -3 are painted versions of girls-as479330034,
guys-as211660510 and guys-as299117684. Lettering in the cards and stickers
uses Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License (Anton, Bangers, Caveat,
Luckiest Guy, Permanent Marker, Playfair Display, Shrikhand, Alfa Slab One,
Abril Fatface, Pacifico, Inter, Inter Tight), which allows pictures made with
the fonts. The emoji in the template screens and the Emoji group are Noto
Color Emoji, saved as PNG. The font is under the SIL Open Font License, and
the same artwork is published under the Apache License 2.0 in Google's
noto-emoji repository. The
Dollar sign is the "$" of Outfit, a Google Font under the SIL Open Font
License, which allows pictures made with the font. The flag stickers draw the
public national flag designs of the United States, the United Kingdom and
Canada.

The Sun, Winking sun, Alarm clock and Warm glow entries point at the files the
Sunup templates already ship in `public/data/projects/` rather than at copies,
so projects made from those templates keep their pictures.
