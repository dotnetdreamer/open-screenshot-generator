# Every device: your screenshots for every store size

Each store wants screenshots in its own sizes: iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and the Mac. Make your screenshots once, and the app remakes them for each of those devices.

To open it, click the phone button in the toolbar and choose **Every device**. It's in the **Preview** menu (the eye) too.

## What you see

On the left is a list of devices, grouped by store: App Store, Google Play and Mac App Store. The size you designed for says **Your original**.

Click a device to see your screenshots remade for it. On a wider screen the headline gets more room, so it often fits on fewer lines, and the phone stays under the text. On the Mac, the text moves to one side and the phone to the other.

**All devices**, at the top, shows every device at once, so you can check everything in one go. Click a row to open that device.

If your project has more than one language, pick a language at the top to see the screenshots in it.

## Pick a layout

Under each screenshot are small pictures of the layouts it can have on that device, made from your own screenshot:

- **Like your design**: the same order as your design, text above or below the phone.
- **Text on the left** and **Text on the right**: the text on one side, the phone on the other. Good for the Mac.
- **Shrink to fit**: your screenshot as it is, made smaller, with space around it.

The one marked **our pick** is the layout the app chose. Click another to use it for that device. Your choice is saved with the project, and **Undo** takes it back.

Some screenshots don't fit a new shape well, for example a lot of text on the short Mac screen. Those are made smaller to fit instead, and the device says how many.

## Screenshots from an iPad or a Mac

At first, the iPad, tablet and Mac screenshots show your app on a phone. We don't stretch a phone screenshot into an iPad shape, because that would cut parts of your app off. Apple also wants iPad screenshots to show your app on an iPad.

To show an iPad, click **Add a screenshot from an iPad** under a screenshot, or drop the picture on it. The phone turns into an iPad that shows your picture. It works the same way for Android tablets and the Mac. Use **Change the iPad screenshot** to swap the picture, or the bin to remove it. A picture taken sideways has its sides cut off, so take it with the iPad upright.

It works the other way too. If you designed for the iPad, your iPad screenshots don't fit an iPhone or Android phone screen, so their edges get cut off. Those phones then ask for a phone screenshot, but only under the screenshots that don't fit.

With a language picked at the top, the picture you add is for that language only. Add one for your main language first, because that one shows in every language that doesn't have its own.

## Export the screenshots

Turn on **Export** next to a device, and its screenshots are exported along with your project. The Export dialog ticks them for you: iPad sizes are under **Additional App Store sizes**, and Google Play and the Mac are under **Other devices**. You can also click **Export** at the bottom of Every device.

When you upload to App Store Connect or Google Play from the desktop app and pick an iPad or tablet size, the screenshots are remade the same way.

## Change one device's screenshots by hand

Click the device, then **Make an iPad 13-inch project** (the button names the device you picked). A new project opens at that size, in every language, and you can change it however you like. Your first project stays the same. The new project doesn't follow changes you make to your first project later.

## Change the whole project to another size

The phone button's **Convert project to** list changes your whole project to another device's size, with the same layouts as Every device. **Undo** changes it back.
