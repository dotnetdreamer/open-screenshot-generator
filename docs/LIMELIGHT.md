# Limelight: film your app in 3D

Limelight makes promo videos of your app. Your screenshots go on real 3D phones and tablets, the light looks like a product photo shoot, and a camera moves around them. You get a video for the App Store, Google Play or social posts.

Open it with **Limelight** on the left, under Editor.

## Start a scene

1. Open the project with your screenshots in the editor first. Limelight puts them on the phones for you.
2. In Limelight, press **Start a scene** (or **New scene**).
3. Pick a camera move, such as **Hero orbit** or **Side by side**. Each card shows your own screenshots. Pick **One phone** if you'd rather build it yourself.

Every change is saved on this computer as you go. Your scenes are listed on the left.

## Move the camera

The camera moves from one **shot** to the next. A shot is where the camera is at a moment in the video.

- Move the playhead on the timeline to a moment.
- Drag the stage to swing the camera. Shift-drag slides it, and scrolling moves it closer or further.
- Let go, and that view is a new shot at that moment. The camera now moves there.

Click a shot (a diamond on the Camera row) to change it on the right: how it moves to the next shot (**Smooth** stops at each shot, **Glide** keeps moving through them), the lens, the blur and where it looks.

**Fit all** moves the camera back until everything is in the picture. **From outside** shows the whole stage with the camera's path, so you can see the move you made. Nothing you do there changes the video.

## Phones, tablets and titles

- Every phone and tablet is its real size, so an iPad next to a phone looks the way it would on a desk.
- Click a phone to pick it, then drag it to move it. Hold Option while you drag to turn it.
- On the right you can change the model, the colour, the pictures on its screen and how it floats or spins.
- **Add** at the top adds another device, a title, or a shot at the playhead.
- To make a phone move during the video, press **Animate from here**, move the playhead, and move the phone again.

## Ask AI

On the Mac and PC apps, with Claude Code or Codex signed in, press **Ask AI** and say what you want, such as "Make the camera go all the way round the phone". You'll see the scene change, and you can undo it.

## Save and share

- **Export video** makes an MP4. The App Store size is 886 x 1920 at 30 frames a second, and App Store previews must be 15 to 30 seconds long. Every video has a silent sound track, which App Store Connect asks for.
- **Save picture** saves the frame at the playhead as a PNG.
- **Save to Drive** keeps the scene and its pictures in your Google Drive, in a folder called Open Screenshot Generator Limelight. **Open from Google Drive** brings a scene back, on this computer or another one. If the scene on this computer has changes the one in Drive doesn't, you get both. Sign in with Google Drive under Account first. Scenes don't save to GitHub, because videos are too big for it.
- **Save as my template**, in a scene's menu on the left, keeps it as a starting point. It shows under Your templates when you start a scene.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Play or pause |
| K | Add a shot at the playhead |
| Left and right arrows | One frame back or on (with Shift, one second) |
| Delete | Delete what's picked |
| Cmd or Ctrl Z | Undo (with Shift, redo) |
